## Design Specificity Verdict

The Surface, Space, Guide, curated brush language, and 3D navigator feel native to this product. The generic icon rail and rounded neutral panels do not yet give the shell a distinctive identity. The larger issue is usability rather than styling: the app is calm at rest and becomes complex as soon as a user opens a panel.

The deterministic scan of `src/App.tsx` returned zero findings. Browser DOM and accessibility inspection found labeled navigation, pressed states, and disabled Undo/Redo, but also exposed the drawing canvas as an image named `nv-canvas`. The clean detector result does not invalidate the UX findings because the main problems cross component and CSS boundaries.

## What Works

- The default brush state is task-first and forgiving: Surface, Flat Paint, ribbon profile, predictive smoothing, and snapping are ready immediately.
- The touch baseline is strong: 44px targets, safe-area positioning, visible focus outlines, and thumb-friendly bottom controls.
- Autosave, work-loss decisions, mutually exclusive sheets, Undo/Redo, and Escape dismissal provide a solid safety foundation.

## Priority Issues

| Before | After | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **P1:** Phone panels are forced full width and can intercept the persistent mode rail. | Reserve a rail gutter and give the visible mode group a deliberate higher stacking context. | Users should switch modes in one tap without closing a sheet. |
| **P1:** Draw and Select expose too many equal-weight choices. | Show the recommended/current choice and 2-3 frequent alternatives; move expert controls under one Advanced disclosure. | Complexity appears exactly when a first-timer asks for more control. |
| **P1:** No visible first-stroke cue appears on the blank canvas. | Add a dismissible, non-modal `Drag to draw on the surface` hint that disappears after the first stroke. | Excellent defaults are ineffective when the canvas does not look drawable. |
| **P1:** Save confidence and Export are buried across multiple surfaces. | Keep a quiet Saved state near the project name and make Export a first-group menu action. | Hobbyists need confidence that work is safe and easy to share. |
| **P2:** Labels such as Gizmo and Soft Selection Falloff leak expert terminology. | Use Move controls and Move nearby strokes as primary labels. | Plain language reduces translation effort without removing power. |

## Cognitive Load and Personas

Cognitive load is high: four of eight checks fail (single focus, chunking, visual hierarchy, minimal choices). The persistent shell exposes about fifteen actions; opened Draw, Select, Add, and More surfaces exceed four choices at several decision points.

- Jordan, first-timer: icon-only side modes, no visible help, and expert vocabulary obstruct the first task.
- Casey, mobile: bottom shortcuts are good, but full-width panels make the mode rail unavailable.
- Sam, accessibility: focus and ARIA foundations are good, but the canvas name is meaningless and visually occluded controls can remain exposed.
- Mina, hobbyist painter: curated brushes invite experimentation, but hidden save/export certainty makes experimentation feel risky.

## Improvement Direction

Fix mobile mode access first. Then teach the first stroke, simplify default panel contents, surface Saved and Export, and finish with plain-language and motion/accessibility cleanup. Preserve advanced tools behind progressive disclosure rather than deleting them.
